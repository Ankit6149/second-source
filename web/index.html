The approach

From observation to a testable answer.

Every stage is inspectable. Observations are facts; hypotheses are possibilities; independent tests decide what matches.

01 / CAPTURE

Observe

Record the actual input, response, error and state, within an authorized boundary.

02 / ORGANIZE

Understand

Separate demonstrated behavior from guessed rules and unresolved unknowns.

03 / DISTILL

Construct

Create a separate scoped candidate with evidence-linked provenance and identity.

04 / EXECUTE

Verify

Compare behavior independently, measure divergence and export auditable results.
